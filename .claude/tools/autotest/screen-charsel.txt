# SCREEN TEST: New Game's character selection on the pad (from Fallout 2, 2026-11-14, „dodáme to i do F1“; his layout:
# LB / RB step the character, A TAKE, X CREATE, Y MODIFY, B BACK, each button with its badge) -- and the crash it closes:
# in there Y swapped hands and LB / RB fired the quick slots of a game that did not exist yet („hra spadla v char selectu
# po zmáčknutí Y“). Opened over the scenario's world (the harness has no title menu); the shots show the badges.
# The premades: 0 Max Stone, 1 Natalia, 2 Albert.
name screen-charsel
map DESERT1.MAP
seed 1
duration 15
dude tile 20100 rot 2

# A goes down before the selector opens and is still held in it, as the A that pressed NEW GAME is: no TAKE from it
pad 900 A 1
open 1000 selector
pad +700 A 0
shot +600
# RB twice, LB once: the character steps 0 -> 1 -> 2 -> 1
tap +700 RB
tap +700 RB
tap +700 LB
shot +600
# Y = MODIFY: the editor opens (the crash was Y in here)
tap +800 Y
shot +1500
# B leaves the editor, still held as the selector comes back: that B must not also BACK out
tap +800 B 400
shot +1500
# B = BACK leaves the selector
tap +800 B
note +1500 the selector should be closed by now

# the checks (run.js)
expect \[FLOW/autotest\] opened screen=selector
expect \[FLOW/charsel\] open premades=3 pad=1
expect \[FLOW/charsel-red\] badged=1
# the pointer: hidden for the pad on entry, and again once the editor (which puts the arrow back) has closed
expect-count 2 \[FLOW/charsel-cursor\] hidden
expect-not \[INPUT/charsel-pad\] button=A
expect-count 2 \[INPUT/charsel-pad\] button=RB
expect-count 1 \[INPUT/charsel-pad\] button=LB
expect \[FLOW/charsel-show\] premade=2
expect-count 3 \[FLOW/charsel-show\] premade=1
expect-count 1 \[INPUT/charsel-pad\] button=Y
expect \[FLOW/autotest\] modal win=\d+ at=\d+,\d+ size=640x480
expect-count 1 \[INPUT/charsel-pad\] button=B
expect \[FLOW/charsel\] close rc=3
expect \[FLOW/autotest\] closed screen=selector rc=3
expect-not \[INPUT/quick-slot
expect-not \[INPUT/hand-swap\]
expect \[FLOW/autotest\] end why=time
